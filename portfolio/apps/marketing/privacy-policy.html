Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sana Hameed ("I", "me", "my") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit sana-freelance.vercel.app (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information I Collect

I collect two types of information:

  • Information you provide voluntarily — such as your name, email address, and message content when you contact me via email, WhatsApp, or the contact form.
  • Information collected automatically — such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and time spent on the Site. This is collected via cookies and analytics tools.

2. How I Use Your Information

I use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide the services you request
  • Improve the Site's content, performance, and user experience
  • Display relevant advertising through Google AdSense
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This Site uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. I use:

  • Essential cookies — required for the Site to function (e.g., remembering your cookie consent choice)
  • Analytics cookies — to understand how visitors use the Site (via Vercel Analytics)
  • Advertising cookies — set by Google AdSense to show relevant ads

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect Site functionality.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

This Site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies via aboutads.info.

5. Analytics

I use Vercel Analytics to collect anonymous usage data such as page views, referrers, and general location (country-level). This data is aggregated and does not personally identify you.

6. Data Sharing

I do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. I only share information with:

  • Service providers (Google AdSense, Vercel) as described above
  • Legal authorities, if required by law

7. Data Retention

Contact form submissions and email correspondence are retained for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which they were collected, typically up to 2 years. Analytics data is retained per Vercel's standard retention policy.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction — request corrections to inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request deletion of your data
  • Objection — object to processing of your data
  • Portability — request transfer of your data

To exercise any of these rights, email me at sanahameed@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact me so I can delete it.

10. International Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own (including the United States, where Google and Vercel servers are located). By using the Site, you consent to these transfers.

11. Security

I take reasonable measures to protect your information, including using HTTPS and reputable service providers. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.
